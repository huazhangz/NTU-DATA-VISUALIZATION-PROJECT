SD6105 · CDC PLACES county data, 2025 release

Adult health across U.S. counties

Five interactive views of CDC estimates for 3,143 U.S. counties. Views 1–3 focus on diabetes, obesity and physical inactivity; views 4–5 widen the lens to all measures. All rates are age-adjusted so counties with older or younger populations can be compared fairly.

1 · Where are rates highest?

County map of diabetes, obesity and physical inactivity. Hover a county for its estimate and range.

2 · Inactivity and diabetes

Each dot is a county: places with more physical inactivity tend to have more diabetes.

3 · Where problems overlap

Counties in the top 25% for none, one, two or all three of the core measures.

4 · What else goes with it?

Pick a measure and see which of the other 2023 measures rise and fall with it.

5 · How does each state compare?

Each state's typical county against the typical U.S. county, for 39 measures.

Source: CDC PLACES County Data (GIS Friendly Format), 2025 release (i46a-9kgh); U.S. Census Bureau 2023 county boundaries. Values are model-based estimates, not direct counts. They describe counties, not individuals, and show association, not cause.
