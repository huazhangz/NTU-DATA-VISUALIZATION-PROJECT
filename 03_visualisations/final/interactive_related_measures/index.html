Read this carefully

These are county estimates from one CDC statistical model, which uses the same county background data (age, income, education and so on) for every measure. That makes many measures look more alike than they really are: † marks correlations of 0.9 or more, which mostly reflect the model, not a direct link. Correlation is not cause. Social-need measures are published for 2,299 counties (not CO, FL, KY, OR, PA, SD, TN, TX, VT, WA, WY); other measures for 2,956 (not KY, PA). Only 2023 estimates are shown.
